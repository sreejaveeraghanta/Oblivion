A game of bluff and deceit

The objective is to be the last person with influence in the game, so don't let your opponents fool you. Play smart and Stay in the game
Bluff, lie anc cheat your way through!

ACTIONS
On each round, you can only do one of the following actions:
- Take a coin as income
- Take two coins as foreign aid
- Claim a role and perform its action

On someone else's turn
- block their action
- challenge their claim on the rule


POSSIBLE ROLES
- Duke: take 3 coins from treasury
- Captain: steal 2 coins from a target player
- Assassin: pay 3 coins to treasury to target a player - target loses influence
- Contenssa: block assassination
- Ambassador: draw two replacement cards and swap out one of your roles with another one from the drawn cards
